About Tattoos by Turtle

Bold urban ink, graffiti flow, and Japanese-inspired motifs — bringing your tattoo visions to life.

Meet Turtle

Hello! I'm Turtle, an urban street art tattoo artist blending graffiti, bold lines, and Japanese-inspired designs. I specialize in creating tattoos that are not only visually striking but also deeply symbolic and personal.

My style combines:

  • Urban graffiti & street art: Vibrant colors, bold outlines, and dynamic compositions.
  • Japanese motifs: Traditional elements, symbolism, and storytelling through ink.
  • Custom flash designs: Regularly created for clients and collectors, available in my flash books.

I currently work at Inksanity Studio but my personal brand, Tattoos by Turtle, allows me to bring my own vision, flash, and merch to the world.
