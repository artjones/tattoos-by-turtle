Deposits & Pricing

A non-refundable deposit is required to secure all tattoo appointments. Deposits go toward the final price of your tattoo and ensure your time is reserved.

  • Deposits are required for all bookings
  • Deposits come off the final session total
  • Deposits are non-refundable
  • Reschedules require at least 48 hours notice
  • No-shows forfeit the deposit

Deposits protect both the artist’s time and the client’s commitment.
